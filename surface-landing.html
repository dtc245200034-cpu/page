Questions

Surface is Microsoft’s family of premium PCs and 2-in-1 devices designed for productivity, creativity, and portability with Windows and Microsoft 365.

Many Surface devices support Xbox Game Pass and cloud gaming. Higher-end models with dedicated graphics offer a better experience for modern titles.

Choose Surface Pro for maximum versatility (tablet + laptop), Surface Laptop for a classic clamshell experience, or Surface Laptop Ultra for high performance creative work.

Some bundles include Microsoft 365. Otherwise it can be purchased separately. Features and availability may vary by region.
